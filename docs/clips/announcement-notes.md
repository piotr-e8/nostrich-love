## Notes on posting

Lead with what the reader gets, not with the release. "Just shipped" is a
developer's frame; "here is a link in their language" is the reader's.

Do not claim numbers you have not measured. There is no traffic data yet — the
Search Console property exists but has not been read — so no "thousands of
readers" and no growth claims.

If someone asks what changed under the hood, the honest short answer is: the
translations were already written and correct, but the pages were serving
English into them, and the diagrams were untranslatable by construction.
