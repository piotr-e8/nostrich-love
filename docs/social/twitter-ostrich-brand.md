## Character Profile

### Who is the Nostrich?

The Nostrich is a friendly, curious purple ostrich who serves as the guide and mascot for nostrich.love. Unlike a corporate brand account, the Nostrich has personality, opinions, and a genuine desire to help people discover Nostr.

**Appearance:** Purple ostrich, friendly expression, approachable and non-threatening
**Vibe:** Helpful teacher meets cool older sibling who knows about Bitcoin
**Knowledge Level:** Knows Nostr inside-out but explains it simply
**Bitcoin Cred:** Genuine Bitcoiner, understands the ethos deeply

---

## Personality Traits

### Core Characteristics

**1. Curious and Helpful**
- Always eager to explain things
- Patient with beginners
- Celebrates small wins
- "You've got this!" energy

**2. Slightly Nerdy but Approachable**
- Uses tech terms but explains them
- Gets excited about cryptography
- Makes Bitcoin analogies
- Not condescending

**3. Bitcoin-Aligned**
- Understands "not your keys, not your coins"
- References Bitcoin philosophy
- Skeptical of centralized platforms
- Values sovereignty and freedom

**4. Not Preachy or Maximalist**
- Doesn't shame people for using Twitter
- Acknowledges Nostr's current limitations
- Honest about trade-offs
- "Try it, see if you like it" attitude

**5. Optimistic but Realistic**
- Believes in Nostr's potential
- Acknowledges UX challenges
- Honest about learning curve
- Focuses on long-term benefits

## Voice & Tone

### Writing Style

**Conversational**
- "Hey there! 👋" not "Welcome to Nostr"
- "Let's figure this out together" not "Here is the documentation"
- "I get it, I was confused too" not "It's actually quite simple"

**Simple Language**
- Avoid jargon unless explaining it
- Use analogies (especially Bitcoin-related)
- Short sentences and paragraphs
- Break complex topics into steps

**Encouraging**
- "You can do this!"
- "Don't worry, everyone asks this"
- "Great question!"
- Celebrate progress

**Bitcoin-Fluent**
- "Your keys, your voice" (like "not your keys, not your coins")
- References to self-custody
- Understanding of decentralization
- Privacy-conscious perspective

**Emoji Usage**
- 🦩 - The ostrich (signature, use liberally)
- 🗝️ - Keys
- 🔐 - Security/privacy
- 🚀 - Growth/excitement
- ✅ - Checklists, benefits
- ❌ - Problems, limitations
- 👇 - "Continue reading below"

**Tone by Situation:**

| Situation | Tone |
|-----------|------|
| Explaining basics | Patient, encouraging, simple |
| Addressing concerns | Empathetic, honest, solution-focused |
| Celebrating wins | Enthusiastic, proud, community-focused |
| Debating/comparisons | Confident but respectful, factual |
| Technical details | Clear, step-by-step, not overwhelming |

## Engagement Rules

### DO:

✅ **Reply to Every Question**
- Build trust through responsiveness
- Even "dumb" questions deserve answers
- Creates a help desk reputation

✅ **Quote Tweet Bitcoiners Complaining About Twitter**
- "There's an alternative..."
- Be helpful, not smug
- Example: Someone says "Twitter shadowbanned me again" → Quote with "Have you tried Nostr? 🦩"

✅ **Share User Success Stories**
- With permission
- Screenshots of DMs (anonymized)
- "This week I helped..."

✅ **Use Polls for Engagement**
- "Have you tried Nostr yet?"
- "What's your biggest concern?"
- "Which feature matters most?"

✅ **Thread Educational Content**
- 3-7 tweets is the sweet spot
- Clear hook in first tweet
- Numbered tweets for easy reading
- Strong CTA in final tweet

✅ **Be Consistent**
- Post 3-5x per week minimum
- Same time windows (experiment to find yours)
- Ongoing threads series ("Nostr 101")

✅ **Engage Authentically**
- Reply to other Nostr educators
- Support the ecosystem
- Be part of the community

### DON'T:

❌ **Spam Hashtags**
- Use 0-2 max per tweet
- Relevant only: #Nostr #Bitcoin #Privacy
- Avoid: #Crypto #Web3 #Blockchain (wrong audience)

❌ **Post More Than 3x/Day**
- Quality over quantity
- Don't overwhelm followers
- Save some content for tomorrow

❌ **Use Automation Tools**
- Risk of shadowban
- Inauthentic engagement
- Twitter hates bots

❌ **Be Negative About Other Protocols**
- Don't bash Mastodon or Bluesky
- Focus on Nostr's benefits
- "Nostr is great because..." not "Mastodon sucks because..."

❌ **Overpromise**
- Don't say Nostr is perfect
- Acknowledge limitations
- Honest about UX challenges

❌ **Use Crypto Jargon**
- No "zk-rollups," "DeFi," "NFTs"
- Keep it Bitcoin/Nostr focused
- Assume audience knows Bitcoin basics but not crypto

❌ **Be Defensive**
- If someone criticizes Nostr, listen
- Acknowledge valid concerns
- Offer solutions, not arguments

## Thread Structure Template

### High-Engagement Thread Format

**Tweet 1: The Hook**
- Contrarian statement OR curiosity gap
- Strong opinion OR surprising fact
- Must make people stop scrolling

**Tweet 2-3: The Problem**
- Relatable pain point
- What people are frustrated about
- Build emotional connection

**Tweet 4-6: The Solution**
- What Nostr offers
- Specific benefits
- How it's different

**Tweet 7: Proof/Example**
- Real user story
- Data/statistics
- Social proof

**Tweet 8: Call to Action**
- Clear next step
- Link or "Reply with questions"
- Make it easy to engage

**Example:**
```
Tweet 1 (Hook):
"Twitter vs Nostr: The 5 differences that matter"

Tweet 2-6 (The Differences):
"1. Your account can be banned → Your account CANNOT be banned
2. Algorithm decides what you see → YOU decide what you see
..."

Tweet 7 (Proof):
"I've helped 50+ Bitcoiners switch. Most say: 'I wish I did this sooner'"

Tweet 8 (CTA):
"Want to try it? Start here: nostrich.love/guides 👇"
```

## Common Scenarios & Responses

### Scenario 1: "Nostr is too complicated"

**Response:**
```
I hear this a lot! Let me ask you:

How long did it take you to understand Bitcoin?

Probably weeks or months, right?

Nostr is actually simpler:
✅ Download app
✅ Create keys
✅ Start posting

5 minutes. The "complexity" comes from it being new, not from it being hard.

Want me to walk you through it? 🦩
```

### Scenario 2: "I don't want to start over with 0 followers"

**Response:**
```
Totally get it. But here's the thing:

Your Twitter followers:
❌ Aren't really yours (Twitter owns them)
❌ Don't all see your posts (algorithm)
❌ Can disappear tomorrow (ban risk)

Nostr followers:
✅ Are portable (same keys on every app)
✅ See every post you make
✅ Can't be taken away

Plus, the Bitcoin community on Nostr is incredibly welcoming. You'll hit 1k faster than you think.

Try it for a week. What do you have to lose? 🦩
```

### Scenario 3: "Why should I trust you?"

**Response:**
```
Great question! You shouldn't trust me blindly.

Here's what I am:
✅ A Bitcoiner who loves Nostr
✅ Running nostrich.love to help beginners
✅ Transparent about affiliation

Here's what I'm NOT:
❌ Affiliated with any Nostr app
❌ Selling anything
❌ Getting paid by Nostr projects

My goal: Help more people discover Nostr. That's it.

Try the guides. If they suck, tell me and I'll fix them. Deal? 🦩
```

### Scenario 4: "What's wrong with Twitter?"

**Response:**
```
Nothing's "wrong" with it if it works for you!

But many Bitcoiners are concerned about:
🔒 Censorship of Bitcoin content
🤖 Algorithm suppressing reach
👤 Ban risk for wrongthink
📊 Platform owning your data

Nostr fixes those issues while keeping what makes social media fun.

Not saying you HAVE to switch. Just letting you know there's an alternative if you want it 🦩
```

## Crisis Management

### If Someone Accuses You of Shilling

**Response:**
```
I get it - lots of shills in crypto.

I'm not selling anything. I run nostrich.love which is free.

I'm just a Bitcoiner who discovered Nostr and wants to help others discover it too.

If the guides help you, great. If not, no hard feelings.

What specific concern do you have? Happy to address it 🦩
```

**Response:**
```
Heads up: Some Nostr apps are experiencing [issue] right now.

This is the growing pains of early tech. Remember when Bitcoin fees were $50+?

The devs are working on it. If you try Nostr today and it doesn't work perfectly, that's why.

DM me if you need help or want updates 🦩
```

```
Thanks to @[user] for catching that. Always learning! 🦩
```
