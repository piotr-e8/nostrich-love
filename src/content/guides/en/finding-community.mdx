## Hashtag Strategy (2 minutes)

### Why Hashtags Matter

Hashtags are the primary way to discover content and people on Nostr:

**Benefits:**

- Find people with shared interests
- Join ongoing conversations
- Get discovered by others
- Build topical authority

### Popular Nostr Hashtags

**General:**

- #introductions - Say hello
- #asknostr - Ask questions
- #nostr - General Nostr discussion

**Technology:**

- #bitcoin - Bitcoin discussion
- #lightning - Lightning Network
- #privacy - Privacy tools
- #opensource - Open source software

**Lifestyle:**

- #photography - Photos
- #music - Music sharing
- #art - Visual art
- #writing - Long-form content
- #foodstr - Food and cooking
- #coffeechain - Coffee culture
- #memes - Memes and humor

**Community & Culture:**

- #grownostr - Gardening and growing
- #plebchain - Bitcoin plebs community

**Regional:**

- #africa, #asia, #europe, #americas
- City-specific tags
- Language tags

### Using Hashtags Effectively

**Do:**

- Use 2-5 relevant hashtags per post
- Check trending tags in your client
- Follow hashtags you're interested in
- Create your own for events/series

**Don't:**

- Spam with 10+ hashtags
- Use irrelevant tags
- Tag every single post
- Create duplicate/similar tags

### Discovering New Hashtags

1. **Check what others use** - See what hashtags popular accounts in your niche use
2. **Search and explore** - Try searching different topics
3. **Ask the community** - "What hashtags should I follow for [topic]?"

<Callout variant="success">
  **Pro tip:** Mix broad tags (#bitcoin) with specific ones (#bitcoinmining) to
  reach different audiences.
</Callout>

## Exploring Relay Feeds (2 minutes)

### Why Browse by Relay?

Hashtags connect you to topics across all of Nostr. But what if you want to discover entire communities organized around specific relays?

**Think of relays as different newspapers:**

- **Your current feed**: People you follow (mixed relays)
- **Relay feed**: Everyone posting to that specific relay

Each relay develops its own personality and community:
- Bitcoin-focused relays discuss ₿ topics
- Art relays showcase creative work  
- Regional relays connect local communities
- Tech relays focus on development

**How to use it:**

1. **Pick a category** - Bitcoin, Art, Music, Tech, Development, Gaming, or Regional
2. **Browse like a newspaper** - See what's happening on each relay
3. **Preview before joining** - Check the relay's content and community
4. **Add to your client** - Follow instructions to add interesting relays

### Recommended First Relay

**Spatia Arcana** - Community-curated topical relay
- URL: `wss://spatia-arcana.com`
- Start here for a taste of relay-based discovery
- It only accepts posts from a set list of writers, so treat it as somewhere to read rather than somewhere to publish

### Which Clients Support This?

**Best for relay browsing:**

- **Jumble** (Web) - Built specifically for relay exploration
- **Gossip** (Desktop) - Desktop app that can show you any single relay's feed
- **Coracle** (Web) - Advanced relay management
- **Snort** (Web) - Feature-rich

Primal is a fine everyday client, but it is not on this list for a reason. Primal
apps read from Primal's own caching server, which gathers events from many relays
and serves them back as one stream, so there is no "show me what is on this
relay" view to open.

**Better to follow 50 great accounts than 5,000 random ones.**

- Have been around a while

- Spam or low-quality posts
- Never engage with others

## Community Directories (2 minutes)

### Start With a Follow Pack

The fastest route is still the [curated follow packs](/follow-pack) on this site.
Pick the topic that sounds like you, skim the accounts, and take the ones you like.
No account needed to look.

### Nostr Explorers

An explorer is a website that reads Nostr and shows it to you without asking you to
log in. Handy when you want to check somebody out before following them, or when a
friend sends you a link and you just want to read it.

Two that work today:

- **primal.net** - search for a topic or a name and read the results in your browser
- **njump.me** - paste any Nostr link (an `npub`, a `note1...`, a `nevent1...`) and it opens as a normal web page

Search a topic you care about, look at who keeps showing up, open a few profiles and
read their last dozen posts. If the voice fits, follow. That is the whole method.

### Client-Specific Discovery

Most clients also have their own "suggested users", trending posts, or topic lists
built in. They are worth a look, though the suggestions tend to repeat the same
well-known accounts, so treat them as a starting point rather than the map.

### Creating Your Own List

**If you're well-connected:**

1. Create a post with recommended follows
2. Use #starterpack or similar tag
3. Help newcomers find good people
4. Build community reputation

## Long-Form Content (2 minutes)

### Beyond Short Posts

Nostr isn't just Twitter-style microblogging. There's a rich ecosystem of long-form content:

**Types of Long-Form:**

- Blog posts
- Essays
- Threads (connected posts)
- Newsletters
- Technical articles

### Finding Long-Form Writers

Two clients are built around articles rather than short posts, so they are the
easiest places to find writers.

**Highlighter** (highlighter.com)

- Web app for reading and publishing long pieces
- You can highlight passages, and your highlights become something others can find and zap
- Supports paid subscriptions, so writers can charge readers directly

**YakiHonne** (yakihonne.com)

- Long-form articles and media, with a portal for creators publishing their own work
- Web app plus native iOS and Android apps, and a desktop build
- Zaps and relay settings built in

### Following Writers

**What to look for:**

- Consistent publishing
- Quality over quantity
- Engagement with readers
- Unique perspectives
- Good signal-to-noise ratio

**Benefits:**

- Deeper content
- Better discussions
- Learn new things
- Build relationships

## DM Etiquette (2 minutes)

### Safety First

Nostr DMs are encrypted, so relays cannot read what you wrote. What leaks around
the message depends on which method your client uses.

- **Older DMs (NIP-04)** hide the text but not the participants. A relay can see that you and one particular person are talking, and how often.
- **Newer DMs (NIP-17)** hide the sender: each message is wrapped and signed by a throwaway key. The recipient is still visible in plain text, because the relay needs to know where to deliver it, and so is the timing.
- **Clients differ.** Two people on different apps may not even be using the same method, and messages sometimes fail to appear across that gap.

Either way, the content is private and the fact that somebody is receiving
messages is not. For the full picture, read
[Private messages with NIP-17](/guides/nip17-private-messages).

### DM Best Practices

**Do:**

- Be respectful
- Introduce yourself
- Keep it relevant
- Respect boundaries
- Remember the message body is private, the surrounding metadata is not

**Don't:**

- Spam or harass
- Send unsolicited messages
- Share private keys (EVER)
- Assume complete privacy
- Send sensitive info

### Handling Unwanted DMs

**If someone bothers you:**

1. **Mute or block** - Both do the same thing on Nostr: your app stops showing
   you that person. It is a filter on your side, not a wall on theirs.
2. **Know what it does not do** - There is no protocol-level block. The person
   is not told, and can still read your public posts, reply to them and mention
   you. Other people still see those replies; you just stop seeing them.
3. **Report** - If it is serious, report them in your client and tell people you
   trust. Moderation on Nostr happens through relays and shared block lists, so
   a paid or moderated relay is the closest thing to real enforcement.
4. **Don't engage** - Feeds trolls

**Prevention:**

- Don't post your DMs are open if you don't mean it
- Be selective about who you respond to
- Trust your instincts

## Building Your Network (3 minutes)

### Engagement Strategy

**The 80/20 Rule:**

- 80% adding value (posts, replies, zaps)
- 20% self-promotion

**Ways to Add Value:**

- Thoughtful replies
- Sharing insights
- Answering questions
- Curating content
- Encouraging others

### Consistency Matters

**Post regularly:**

- Daily is great
- Few times a week is good
- Weekly minimum
- Quality over quantity

**Best times to post:**

- Varies by your audience
- Experiment and see
- Generally: morning/evening
- Avoid middle of night (unless that's your timezone)

### Building Relationships

**One-on-one:**

- Reply to their posts
- Zap their content
- Send thoughtful DMs
- Remember details
- Be genuine

**Community:**

- Participate in conversations
- Help newcomers
- Share opportunities
- Celebrate others' wins

### Patience

**It takes time:**

- First 100 followers are hardest
- Momentum builds slowly
- Quality relationships > quantity
- Be in it for the long term

<Callout variant="info">
  **Community > Audience.** Build genuine connections, not just follower counts.
</Callout>
